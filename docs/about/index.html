Details

I am a Dual Degree student in Engineering Design at IIT Madras. My work sits mainly around artificial intelligence, machine learning, deep learning and software systems. I like projects where modelling choices, data pipelines and engineering constraints have to come together in one usable system.

A recurring theme in my work is adaptability. I have worked on real-time gesture recognition, few-shot learning, multilingual voice interaction, retrieval-backed assistants, neural network compiler tooling and applied data systems. I also enjoy building lower-level software and simulation tools from first principles, which has shaped how I think about performance and system design.

Outside my core AI and software work, I have explored data analytics and quantitative research through competitions and research platforms. I treat that as a supporting discipline that sharpens probabilistic thinking, experimentation and evaluation.
